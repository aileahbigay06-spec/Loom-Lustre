About the Model

I created Loom & Lustre as the space where fashion, identity, and digital design intersect. What I wear is not random — it is intentional. Every piece reflects mood, mindset, and growth.

My design philosophy mirrors the way I present myself to the world: structured yet fluid, minimal yet expressive, dark yet refined. I believe clothing and design both speak before we do.

Through Loom & Lustre, I transform personal style into visual storytelling — crafting experiences that are not just seen, but felt.
